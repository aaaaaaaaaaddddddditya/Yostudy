Mathematics Formulas

Arithmetic

Simple Interest Formula

Formula:
SI = (P × R × T) / 100
Where:

P = Principal, R = Rate per annum, T = Time in years

Example:

If P = ₹1000, R = 10%, T = 2 years → SI = ₹200

Percentage Formula

Formula:
Percentage = (Part / Whole) × 100
Shortcut:

Move decimal 2 places to the right and add %

Profit and Loss

Profit % = ((SP - CP) / CP) × 100
Loss % = ((CP - SP) / CP) × 100

Geometry

Area Formulas

Circle:
A = πr²
Triangle:
A = (1/2) × base × height
Rectangle:
A = length × width

Perimeter Formulas

Circle (Circumference):
C = 2πr or πd
Rectangle:
P = 2(length + width)

Algebra

Quadratic Equation

Formula:
x = [-b ± √(b² - 4ac)] / 2a
For ax² + bx + c = 0

Algebraic Identities

(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²
(a - b)² = a² - 2ab + b²
a² - b² = (a + b)(a - b)

Trigonometry

Basic Trigonometric Ratios

sin θ = Opposite / Hypotenuse
cos θ = Adjacent / Hypotenuse
tan θ = Opposite / Adjacent

English Dictionary Sample

📌 Serendipity

Pronunciation: ser-uhn-dip-i-tee

Meaning: The occurrence of events by chance in a happy or beneficial way

Example: Finding that old photograph was pure serendipity.

📌 Eloquent

Pronunciation: el-uh-kwuhnt

Meaning: Fluent or persuasive in speaking or writing

Example: The speaker gave an eloquent speech.

📌 Ephemeral

Pronunciation: ih-fem-er-uhl

Meaning: Lasting for a very short time

Example: The beauty of cherry blossoms is ephemeral.

📌 Ambiguous

Pronunciation: am-big-yoo-uhs

Meaning: Open to more than one interpretation; unclear

Example: The instructions were ambiguous.
