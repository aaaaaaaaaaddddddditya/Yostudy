Physics Formulas

Motion Equations

Velocity-Time:
v = u + at
Displacement:
s = ut + (1/2)at²
Final Velocity:
v² = u² + 2as
Where:

u = initial velocity, v = final velocity, a = acceleration, t = time, s = displacement

Force and Newton's Laws

Newton's Second Law:
F = ma
Weight:
W = mg
Friction Force:
f = μN

Energy and Work

Kinetic Energy:
KE = (1/2)mv²
Potential Energy:
PE = mgh
Work Done:
W = F × s × cosθ
Power:
P = W/t

Light and Optics

Speed of Light:
c = λν (speed = wavelength × frequency)
Lens Formula:
1/f = 1/v + 1/u
Magnification:
m = v/u

Electricity

Ohm's Law:
V = IR
Electric Power:
P = VI = I²R = V²/R
Resistance:
R = ρL/A

Chemistry Concepts

⚛️ Atomic Structure

Electrons: Negatively charged particles orbiting nucleus

Protons: Positively charged particles in nucleus

Neutrons: Neutral particles in nucleus

Atomic Number: Number of protons = Number of electrons

Mass Number: Number of protons + Number of neutrons

🧪 Chemical Reactions

Combination: A + B → AB

Decomposition: AB → A + B

Displacement: AB + C → AC + B

Redox: Transfer of electrons occurs

🌊 Acids and Bases

pH Scale: 0-7 (Acidic), 7 (Neutral), 7-14 (Basic)

Strong Acids: HCl, H₂SO₄, HNO₃

Strong Bases: NaOH, KOH, Ca(OH)₂

Neutral: pH = 7 (Pure water)

Mole Concept

Molar Mass:

Sum of atomic masses of all atoms in a molecule

Number of Moles:
n = mass / molar mass
Avogadro's Number:

1 mole = 6.022 × 10²³ particles

Advanced Physics (150+ Formulas)

Mechanics - Kinematics & Dynamics

v = u + at | s = ut + ½at² | v² = u² + 2as
F = ma | F = dp/dt | Weight W = mg
Friction: f = μN | Impulse: J = FΔt = Δp
Momentum: p = mv | Conservation: p₁ = p₂
Elastic collision: e = (v₂-v₁)/(u₁-u₂) = 1
Inelastic collision: e < 1 | Perfectly inelastic: e = 0
Centripetal Force: F = mv²/r = mω²r
Circular motion: a = v²/r | T = 2πr/v

Work, Energy & Power

Work: W = F·s·cosθ | Kinetic Energy: KE = ½mv²
Potential Energy: PE = mgh | Elastic PE = ½kx²
Total Mechanical Energy: E = KE + PE
Power: P = W/t | P = F·v | Efficiency = (Output/Input)×100
Work-Energy Theorem: W = ΔKE

Gravitation

Newton's Law: F = GMm/r² | g = GM/R²
Orbital velocity: v = √(GM/r)
Escape velocity: v_e = √(2GM/R) = √(2gR)
Gravitational PE: PE = -GMm/r
Kepler's 3rd Law: T² ∝ r³

Rotation - Rigid Body Motion

Angular displacement: θ = s/r | Angular velocity: ω = v/r
Angular acceleration: α = ω/t | Torque: τ = F×r = Iα
Moment of Inertia: I = mr² | Angular momentum: L = Iω
Rotational KE: KE = ½Iω²
Conservation of Angular Momentum: L₁ = L₂

Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM)

Displacement: y = A sin(ωt + φ)
Velocity: v = Aω cos(ωt + φ) = ±ω√(A²-y²)
Acceleration: a = -ω²y | Period: T = 2π/ω
Energy: E = ½mω²A² = ½kA²
Pendulum: T = 2π√(l/g) | Spring: T = 2π√(m/k)

Waves & Oscillations

Wave equation: v = fλ = λ/T
Intensity: I = P/A | I ∝ (Amplitude)²
Doppler Effect: f' = f(v + v_observer)/(v - v_source)
Superposition: y = y₁ + y₂
Standing waves: λ = 2L/n | f = nv/2L

Heat & Thermodynamics (80+ Formulas)

Temperature & Heat:
Heat: Q = mcΔT | Specific Heat Capacity: c = Q/(mΔT)
Latent Heat: Q = mL | Linear Expansion: l = l₀(1 + αΔT)
Volume Expansion: V = V₀(1 + γΔT) | γ = 3α
Gas Laws: PV = nRT | P₁V₁/T₁ = P₂V₂/T₂

Thermodynamics

First Law: ΔU = Q - W | Internal Energy: U = nC_vT
Work by gas: W = PΔV | Isothermal: W = nRT ln(V₂/V₁)
Adiabatic: PVᵞ = constant | TVᵞ⁻¹ = constant
Efficiency: η = (W/Q_h) × 100% | Carnot: η = 1 - T_c/T_h
Entropy: ΔS = Q/T | Second Law: ΔS_universe > 0

Electrostatics

Coulomb's Law: F = kQ₁Q₂/r² = Q₁Q₂/(4πε₀r²)
Electric Field: E = F/q | E = kQ/r²
Electric Potential: V = kQ/r | V = W/q
Potential Difference: V_AB = V_A - V_B = W/q
Capacitor: C = Q/V | C = ε₀A/d
Capacitors: Series 1/C_eq = 1/C₁ + 1/C₂ | Parallel C_eq = C₁ + C₂
Energy stored: U = ½QV = ½CV² = ½Q²/C

Current & Circuits

Current: I = Q/t | Drift velocity: v_d = I/(nAe)
Resistance: R = ρl/A | Resistivity: ρ = RA/l
Ohm's Law: V = IR | Power: P = VI = I²R = V²/R
Resistors: Series R_eq = R₁ + R₂ | Parallel 1/R_eq = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂
EMF & Internal Resistance: V = E - Ir
Kirchhoff's Laws: ΣI_in = ΣI_out | ΣV = 0 (loop)

Magnetism

Magnetic Force: F = BIl = qvB | F = BIl sinθ
Magnetic Field: B = μ₀I/(2πr) | B = μ₀nI (solenoid)
Flux: Φ = BA = BA cosθ | Faraday's Law: E = -dΦ/dt
Lenz's Law: Induced current opposes change
Motional EMF: E = Blv | Transformer: V₁/V₂ = N₁/N₂

Optics & Light

Refractive Index: n = c/v | Snell's Law: n₁sinθ₁ = n₂sinθ₂
Critical Angle: sinθ_c = n₂/n₁ | Total Internal Reflection: θ ≥ θ_c
Lens Formula: 1/f = 1/v + 1/u | Magnification: m = v/u = h'/h
Power of Lens: P = 1/f (in diopters) | Lens Maker: 1/f = (n-1)(1/R₁ - 1/R₂)
Double Slit: y = nλD/d | Diffraction: a sinθ = nλ
Young's Modulus: Y = stress/strain = (F/A)/(Δl/l)

Modern Physics

Photoelectric Effect: hf = KE + Φ | E = hf
De Broglie Wavelength: λ = h/p = h/(mv)
Bohr's Model: E_n = -13.6/n² eV | r_n = 0.53n²/Z Å
Mass-Energy: E = mc²
Half-life: N = N₀(1/2)ⁿ | Activity: A = λN

Chemistry (200+ Formulas & Concepts)

Atomic Structure & Bonding

Atomic Number = Number of Protons = Number of Electrons
Mass Number = Protons + Neutrons
Atomic Mass Unit (amu) = 1/12 of Carbon-12
Molar Mass = Average Atomic Mass (g/mol)
Electronegativity difference: 0-0.4 (Covalent), 0.4-1.7 (Polar Covalent), >1.7 (Ionic)
Oxidation Number Rules: Element = 0, Monatomic ion = Charge, O usually -2, H usually +1

Mole Concept

Molar Mass (M) = Mass of 1 mole in grams
Number of moles (n) = mass/M = V/V_m
Avogadro's Number (N_A) = 6.022 × 10²³
Number of particles = n × N_A
Equivalent weight = Atomic weight/Valency
Normality = Equivalent/Liter of solution

Chemical Equations & Reactions

Types of Reactions:
Combination: A + B → AB
Decomposition: AB → A + B
Displacement: AB + C → AC + B
Double Displacement: AB + CD → AD + CB
Redox: Involves electron transfer (oxidation-reduction)
Acid-Base: H⁺ + OH⁻ → H₂O

Stoichiometry

From balanced equation: mol₁/coeff₁ = mol₂/coeff₂
Limiting reagent = Produces least product
Theoretical yield from limiting reagent
% Yield = (Actual/Theoretical) × 100
% Purity = (Pure substance mass/Total mass) × 100

Acids & Bases

pH = -log[H⁺] | pOH = -log[OH⁻]
pH + pOH = 14 (at 25°C)
Strong Acids: HCl, HBr, HI, HNO₃, H₂SO₄, HClO₄
Strong Bases: NaOH, KOH, Ca(OH)₂, Ba(OH)₂
K_w = [H⁺][OH⁻] = 10⁻¹⁴
K_a = [H⁺][A⁻]/[HA] | K_b = [OH⁻][HB⁺]/[B]
Buffer: pH = pK_a + log([A⁻]/[HA])

Oxidation-Reduction

Oxidation: Loss of electrons | Reduction: Gain of electrons
Oxidation Number rules: Free element = 0
Oxidizing agent (oxidant) = Substance reduced | Reducing agent (reductant) = Substance oxidized
Balancing redox equations: By ion-electron method or oxidation number method

Solutions & Concentrations

Molarity (M) = moles/Liter
Molality (m) = moles/kg of solvent
% w/w = (mass solute/mass solution) × 100
% w/v = (mass solute/volume solution in mL) × 100
% v/v = (volume solute/volume solution) × 100
Normality (N) = Equivalents/Liter
Dilution: M₁V₁ = M₂V₂

Thermochemistry

Enthalpy Change: ΔH = H_products - H_reactants
Exothermic: ΔH < 0 (releases heat)
Endothermic: ΔH > 0 (absorbs heat)
Hess's Law: ΔH_reaction = ΣΔH_products - ΣΔH_reactants
Heat of reaction: q = mcΔT

Chemical Kinetics

Rate = Δ[Concentration]/Δt = Δc/Δt
Rate Law: Rate = k[A]ᵐ[B]ⁿ
Order of reaction = m + n
First Order: ln[A] = ln[A]₀ - kt | t₁/₂ = 0.693/k
Second Order: 1/[A] = 1/[A]₀ + kt | t₁/₂ = 1/(k[A]₀)
Activation Energy: ln(k₂/k₁) = (E_a/R)(1/T₁ - 1/T₂)

Equilibrium

K_c = [Products]/[Reactants] (at equilibrium)
K_p = Partial pressures at equilibrium
Q = Ion product (same as K_c expression)
If Q < K: Reaction goes forward | If Q > K: Reaction goes backward
Le Chatelier's Principle: System shifts to counteract changes
K_a × K_b = K_w (for conjugate acid-base pairs)

Electrochemistry

Oxidation-Reduction at electrodes
Cathode: Reduction occurs (e⁻ gained)
Anode: Oxidation occurs (e⁻ lost)
Faraday's Law: Q = nF | n = Q/(F) moles electrons
Faraday constant F = 96500 C/mol
Standard Cell Potential: E°_cell = E°_cathode - E°_anode
Nernst Equation: E = E° - (0.0592/n) log Q

Organic Chemistry - Functional Groups

Alkane: Saturated HC (C-C single bonds)
Alkene: Unsaturated HC (C=C double bond)
Alkyne: Unsaturated HC (C≡C triple bond)
Alcohol: -OH group | Aldehyde: -CHO group
Ketone: C=O (internal) | Carboxylic Acid: -COOH
Ester: -COOR | Ether: R-O-R | Amine: -NH₂
Isomerism: Same molecular formula, different structure

Polymer Chemistry

Monomer: Smallest unit | Polymer: Many monomers linked
Addition Polymerization: Direct linking (e.g., Polyethylene)
Condensation Polymerization: With byproduct loss (e.g., Polyester)
Degree of Polymerization: Number of monomer units in chain

Biology (150+ Concepts)

Cell Biology

Prokaryotic cell: No nucleus (Bacteria, Archaea)
Eukaryotic cell: Has nucleus (Animals, Plants, Fungi)
Organelles: Nucleus, Mitochondria, Chloroplast, ER, Golgi, Lysosome, Ribosome
Photosynthesis: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + light → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂
Cellular Respiration: C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + Energy (ATP)
Fermentation: Glucose → Lactate (anaerobic)

Genetics & Heredity

DNA Structure: Double helix with base pairs (A-T, G-C)
Chromosome: Structure carrying DNA
Gene: Segment of DNA coding for protein
Allele: Different forms of gene
Genotype: Genetic makeup | Phenotype: Observable traits
Mendel's Laws: Law of Segregation, Law of Independent Assortment, Law of Dominance
Punnett Square: Tool for predicting offspring
Dihybrid Cross: Two traits (9:3:3:1 ratio)

Human Anatomy - Body Systems

Skeletal System:
206 bones in adult human | Functions: Support, Protection, Movement
Muscular System:
3 types: Skeletal (voluntary), Cardiac (heart), Smooth (involuntary)
Circulatory System:
Heart pumps blood | Arteries carry away | Veins return
Blood components: RBC, WBC, Platelets, Plasma
Respiratory System:
Gas exchange in lungs | Diaphragm controls breathing
Digestive System:
Breaks down food: Mouth → Esophagus → Stomach → Small intestine → Colon
Nervous System:
Brain, Spinal cord, Nerves | Functions: Control, Coordination
Endocrine System:
Glands produce hormones | Controls growth, metabolism, reproduction

Plant Physiology

Photosynthesis: Light-dependent & Light-independent reactions
Transpiration: Water loss from leaves
Translocation: Movement of sugars in phloem
Plant Hormones: Auxin, Gibberellin, Cytokinin, Ethylene, Abscisic acid

Ecology & Ecosystems

Ecosystem: Community of organisms + environment
Food Chain: Producer → Consumer → Decomposer
Food Web: Multiple interconnected food chains
Energy Flow: 10% passes each trophic level
Biomes: Tundra, Taiga, Temperate, Desert, Grassland, Tropical
Succession: Ecological change over time
Biodiversity: Variety of organisms

Evolution & Classification

Evolution: Change in species over time
Darwin's Theory: Natural selection, Survival of the fittest
Evidence: Fossils, Anatomical, Molecular
Classification: Kingdom → Phylum → Class → Order → Family → Genus → Species

Reproduction & Development

Mitosis: Cell division producing identical cells (2n → 2n)
Meiosis: Cell division producing gametes (2n → n)
Fertilization: Sperm + Egg → Zygote
Embryonic stages: Zygote → Morula → Blastula → Gastrula → Organogenesis

Molecular Biology

Central Dogma: DNA → RNA → Protein
Transcription: DNA → mRNA (in nucleus)
Translation: mRNA → Protein (in ribosome)
Genetic Code: 64 codons (61 amino acids, 3 stop)
Mutation: Change in DNA sequence

Biotechnology & Immunology

Genetic Engineering: Altering DNA
PCR: Polymerase Chain Reaction (DNA amplification)
Immunity: Innate & Acquired
Antibodies: Proteins fighting antigens
Vaccine: Stimulates immune response without disease
