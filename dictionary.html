English Dictionary Sample

📌 Serendipity

Pronunciation: ser-uhn-dip-i-tee

Meaning: The occurrence of events by chance in a happy or beneficial way

Example: Finding that old photograph was pure serendipity.

📌 Eloquent

Pronunciation: el-uh-kwuhnt

Meaning: Fluent or persuasive in speaking or writing

Example: The speaker gave an eloquent speech.

📌 Ephemeral

Pronunciation: ih-fem-er-uhl

Meaning: Lasting for a very short time

Example: The beauty of cherry blossoms is ephemeral.

📌 Ambiguous

Pronunciation: am-big-yoo-uhs

Meaning: Open to more than one interpretation; unclear

Example: The instructions were ambiguous.

📌 Benevolent

Pronunciation: buh-nev-uh-lent

Meaning: Kind and generous

Example: She is a benevolent person who helps the poor.

📌 Ubiquitous

Pronunciation: yoo-bik-wi-tus

Meaning: Present, appearing, or found everywhere

Example: Mobile phones are ubiquitous in modern society.

📌 Meticulous

Pronunciation: muh-tik-yuh-lus

Meaning: Showing great attention to detail; very careful and precise

Example: She is meticulous about her work.

📌 Pragmatic

Pronunciation: prag-mat-ik

Meaning: Dealing with things in a realistic and practical way

Example: We need a pragmatic approach to this problem.

📌 Diligent

Pronunciation: dil-uh-jent

Meaning: Having or showing care in one's work or duties

Example: A diligent student always completes assignments on time.

📌 Verbose

Pronunciation: ver-bos

Meaning: Using or containing more words than necessary

Example: His verbose writing style was difficult to read.

📌 Pragmatic

Pronunciation: prag-mat-ik

Meaning: Concerned with practical consequences rather than theory

Example: We adopted a pragmatic approach to solving the issue.

📌 Tenacious

Pronunciation: tuh-nay-shus

Meaning: Holding firmly to something; persistent

Example: Her tenacious effort helped her win the competition.

Hindi Dictionary Sample

📌 सुंदर (Sundar)

अर्थ: सुंदर दिखने वाला, आकर्षक

उदाहरण: यह सुंदर बाग है।

📌 ज्ञान (Gyan)

अर्थ: शिक्षा, समझ, विद्या

उदाहरण: ज्ञान शक्ति है।

📌 साहस (Sahas)

अर्थ: डर न होना, वीरता

उदाहरण: उसके साहस की सभी प्रशंसा करते हैं।

📌 मेहनत (Mehnat)

अर्थ: कड़ी परिश्रम, मजदूरी

उदाहरण: मेहनत का फल हमेशा मीठा होता है।

📌 प्रेम (Prem)

अर्थ: प्यार, स्नेह

उदाहरण: माता का प्रेम अतुलनीय होता है।

📌 परिवार (Parivar)

अर्थ: परिजन, घर के सदस्य

उदाहरण: परिवार ही जीवन का आधार है।

📌 नैतिकता (Naitikata)

अर्थ: सदाचार, सही-गलत का ज्ञान

उदाहरण: नैतिकता सबसे बड़ी शिक्षा है।

📌 संघर्ष (Sangharsh)

अर्थ: कठिनाई, लड़ाई

उदाहरण: जीवन का संघर्ष हमें मजबूत बनाता है।
